Copy the calibration files of the camera that made the recording into this folder.

For AH2_thermBL1.ravi (camera serial 25074068, 53 deg lens, range -20..100 C) the file needed is

    Kennlinie-25074068-53-M20-100.prn

(optionally also Cali-25074068-53-M20-100.dat and Cali-25074068.xml, used only by PIX Connect).

Where to find them: on the PC that was connected to the camera, in
    C:\Users\<user>\AppData\Roaming\Imager\Cali\
or pull them from the camera with PIX Connect: Tools -> Extended -> Calibration data -> From device.

Then run:   python python\add_calibration.py AH2_thermBL1.h5
